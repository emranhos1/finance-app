## DFMS v1.4.1

### ✨ New
- **Update from the Dashboard:** if you log in without updating, an "Update to x.y.z" item blinks in the sidebar under Admin Panel. Click it to download and start the update; a thin progress bar shows how far it is.

### 🎨 Improvements
- The "new version available" notice on the Login screen is now a single short line across the window: the text, "What's new?" and "Update now" side by side in the middle.
- The low-balance reminder on the Dashboard is shorter and reads properly: "<account> balance is low: ৳ 20.00 (alert limit ৳ 1,000.00)."

### 🐞 Fixes
- Bangla names in bold text showed as empty boxes (Dashboard reminders, account cards, table chips, the security question, the loan history title). They show correctly now.
