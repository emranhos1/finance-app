## DFMS v1.3.2

### 🐞 Fixes
- **Monthly Income & Expense:** the restore (↻) and edit (pen) buttons were too big in v1.3.1 and made every row of the table taller. They are back to their normal size, so the table is shorter again. They keep their colours (green and blue).
- **Dashboard window size:** the window now opens at 80% of the screen width and at most 90% of the screen height, instead of filling almost the whole screen height.
